This local folder may retain the original asset bundle. For a public clone, supply assets that you are authorized to use at the paths referenced by src/assets.js and convert_captain_glb.py. Review ASSET_CREDITS.md, ENVIRONMENT_ASSET_INVENTORY.md, and MONSTER_ASSET_INVENTORY.md before selecting assets. setup_assets.py installs the Three.js runtime and builds the local compatibility derivatives; it does not download the excluded art files.
